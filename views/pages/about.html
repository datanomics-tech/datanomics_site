Our story

Why we exist

Rwanda is digitising fast. Businesses, institutions and public bodies are moving services online, collecting more data than ever and facing threats that did not exist five years ago. Too often they have to stitch together a hardware supplier, a web agency, a security consultant and a data team that never talk to each other.

DATANOMICS TECHNOLOGIES Ltd was registered in Kigali on 28 June 2024 to close that gap. We are a computer consultancy and facilities-management company that designs, builds, secures and runs technology end to end.

Today that work is organised into four divisions covering everything from branding and digital marketing to ERP systems, connectivity, smart automation and ICT training. Whatever we build, we want our clients to finish the project more confident than when they started.

Our work sits where four disciplines meet: innovation in how services are built, digital transformation of everyday operations, AI and data science that turn records into decisions, and cybersecurity that protects all of it.
